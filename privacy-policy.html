Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This is a plain-language explanation of what this site collects and doesn't collect. It's written to be actually readable, not to bury the important part in legal boilerplate — though if you're relying on this page for a legal or compliance purpose, treat it as a starting point and not a substitute for advice from an actual attorney.

The short version: we never ask for your Duke Energy account number, card number, bank details, Social Security number, or any billing credential. Every payment action described in the guide sends you directly to Duke Energy's own official domain or phone line — nothing routes through us.

Third-party links

This guide links out to Duke Energy's official site and to state utility commission sites. Once you leave this domain, that destination site's own privacy policy applies — we have no control over and no visibility into what those sites collect.

If you contact us

If you submit a correction, question, or message through the Contact Us page, we keep only what's needed to respond to you, and we don't sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We don't expect to change it often, since the underlying fact — that we don't handle your billing data — isn't likely to change.
