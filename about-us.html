About This Site

This site is run by a small independent editorial desk that researches and fact-checks how U.S. utility companies handle billing and payment — starting with Duke Energy, because it's one of the most-searched and, honestly, one of the most spam-infested topics in the space. We are not Duke Energy, a subsidiary of Duke Energy, a payment processor, a call center, or a partner of any kind. Nobody here can see, touch, or modify your Duke Energy account.

Why this guide exists

Search "Duke Energy bill pay" and you'll find dozens of pages listing fake support numbers, guessing at fees, and inventing precise disconnection deadlines nobody actually verified against a real source. We built this guide as a corrective: every number in it is either sourced to Duke Energy's own domain, to on-the-record reporting, or clearly labeled as uncertain when the sources genuinely disagree. If we don't know something for sure, we say so instead of filling the gap with something that merely sounds authoritative.

What we actually do

  • Cross-check phone numbers, addresses, and payment mechanics against duke-energy.com and independent news coverage before publishing them.
  • Flag spam and scam patterns we encounter during research — including fake customer-service numbers that circulate in search results and PDFs.
  • Update the guide when Duke Energy's processes, fees, or tools change.
  • Route every actual payment, account question, or service request back to Duke Energy's own official channels — we never ask for your account number, card details, or bank information.

Not affiliated, not sponsored. We hold no partnership, advertising, or data-sharing arrangement with Duke Energy Corporation. Any Duke Energy trademarks referenced here belong to Duke Energy and are used only descriptively, to help you find their real services.

A note on trust

If you find something in this guide that's wrong, out of date, or that doesn't match what Duke Energy actually told you, we want to know — see the Contact page. A guide like this is only as good as its willingness to be corrected.
